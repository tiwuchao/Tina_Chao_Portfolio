Graphic design

Lighthouse Productions identity · 2026 · Canva

The club had never made merch, so I made some

I sketched seven logo directions and shared them in our members' group chat to see which one people reacted to most. #6 won: a simple tower with light breaking out on both sides. I redrew it in Canva, turned it into a sweatshirt, set up the order form, and handled sales and delivery myself. We sold 46, to members and to audience members.
