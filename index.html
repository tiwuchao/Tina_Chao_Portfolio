Internship · 2026 in2being

From “clients are confused” to better questions at onboarding

Clients of this medical device consultancy didn't consistently understand what it could do, and often trusted its advice only after a misstep had cost them. I interviewed 12 people across the firm and found six kinds of client confusion. The team now uses those areas to guide its questions when onboarding new clients.

Role
UX research, strategy, interaction design
When
Internship · June to August 2026
Tools
Figma, FigJam, PowerPoint, AI-assisted prototyping
  • 16+ interviews with 12 people synthesized into a six-part taxonomy of client confusion
  • A nine-stage client journey tool, a working prototype with device-class and plain-English toggles
  • A usability plan with five participant segments and ten tasks, ready to run

Case study · 2025–26 Argus Farm Stop

Making a farm stop feel easier to choose

Argus had a strong local-food mission and 200+ Michigan farms and food producers on its shelves, but University of Michigan students weren't shopping there. We asked students why, and found that convenience, not price, quality, or even sustainability, shaped their grocery decisions most.

Role
UX researcher · team of 3
When
Class project, August to December 2025
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Canva, Miro, Zoom, WAVE, Claude
  • 9 student interviews and 6 usability tests, synthesized in Miro into four patterns and a student persona
  • A website usability audit that I led independently, with an accessibility check and a competitor benchmark
  • Prioritized recommendations for Argus Farm Stop's marketing manager

Class project · 2024 LendMate Alum

Finding out what kind of alumni would actually lend to students

A peer-to-peer lending startup had a strong pitch for students and no validated picture of the alumni who would fund the loans. We interviewed 30 alumni instead of surveying them, and found two lender mindsets that one onboarding flow couldn't serve.

Role
UX researcher

Process

The highway

These five signs are how I work. Each stop shows where it happened in one of my case studies, and each case study is its own drive, marked mile by mile.

  1. Mile 1Sit with the problemRead, ask, and resist the rush to a solution.

    On the roadAt in2being, the brief said clients didn't value the firm. Sitting with it showed they couldn't see how the work connected.

  2. Mile 2Listen to real peopleResearch from real voices, down to the details people mention in passing.

    On the roadFor LendMate we chose interviews over surveys, because we needed the stories behind the answers.

  3. Mile 3Find the insightThe one idea that keeps coming back, interview after interview, until I can say it in a sentence.

    On the road16+ interviews with 12 people kept circling the same six kinds of confusion. That became the in2being taxonomy.

  4. Mile 4Admit what I got wrongSay where my first read was off and what changed my mind.

    On the roadRecruiting LendMate alumni through our own networks skewed the sample. Next time I'd recruit wider, earlier.

  5. Mile 5Make it work for themNot what's impressive. Whether it works for the person it was made for.

    On the roadStudents already cared about shopping at Argus. So I didn't recommend more convincing. I recommended quicker reorders and clearer pickup, things that fit a busy week.

About
